Team shot profile

Model rates are recency-weighted, shrunk to league average and are what the projection uses. Other columns are season-to-date totals.

Formula

exp_SA = LG × (TeamSA/LG)a × (OppSF/LG)b × home × playoff × b2b × opponent b2b × exp(−gs × logit(win prob)), the game-script multiplier capped at ±12%.

saves ~ Normal(mean = exp_SA × share × sv, var = sv²(μ + μ²/K) + μ·sv(1−sv)), μ = exp_SA × share: shots against vary like a negative binomial and each shot is saved with probability sv, so heavier workloads (underdogs) get a wider spread. P(hit) is then calibrated on graded real lines (side-split, no intercept), blended 60/40 with the no-vig DraftKings price when both sides are posted, and a play needs that probability above the price's break-even + 2%.

Projected saves = exp_SA × time-in-net share × save%   Edge = Projected − Line

What each piece does

  • Team shots against / opponent shots for: recency-weighted per-game SOG, shrunk to league average early on. They replace the old fixed 60/40 blend with fitted weights a and b.
  • Time-in-net share: covers pull risk. Early hooks and injuries are why raw SA/G overstates saves, and this replaces the flat ~12% empty-net haircut.
  • Save%: shrunk toward .900 with a prior of shots, so a hot 10 games doesn't swing the number.
  • Home, playoff and back-to-back: multipliers measured from 2024-25 shot volume.
  • Between seasons: team shot rates keep of last year's weight, because rosters turn over. Early-season numbers lean on the league average and firm up after about 15 games.

Bet rules (your markers)

  • No bet under 1.0 saves of edge (PASS). 1.5+ is the standard play. 2.5+ is HIGH confidence.
  • Green rows are OVER, red rows are UNDER.
  • Confirm the starter (morning skate or warmups) before locking. Every projection is for the goalie shown.
  • Overtime inflates raw saves past the per-60 projection. In a tight, high-event series, lean OVER on close calls.
  • Play-by-play extras (GSAx/60, rebound%, block%, CF%, xGF%) are on the Goalies and Teams tabs for reading a matchup. They aren't in the formula yet, and any that go in will only be added if they improve out-of-sample accuracy.
