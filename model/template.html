How picks work

    Formula

    exp_SA = LG × (TeamSA/LG)a × (OppSF/LG)b × home × playoff × b2b × opponent b2b × exp(−gs × logit(win prob)), the game-script multiplier capped at ±12%.

    saves ~ Normal(mean = exp_SA × share × sv, var = sv²(μ + μ²/K) + μ·sv(1−sv)), μ = exp_SA × share: shots against vary like a negative binomial and each shot is saved with probability sv, so heavier workloads (underdogs) get a wider spread. P(hit) is then calibrated on graded real lines (side-split, no intercept), blended with the market's no-vig price at a weight fitted on graded lines (Calibration tab); whether it is a play follows the rules under How picks work.

    Projected saves = exp_SA × time-in-net share × save% + rolling bias   Edge = Projected − Line

    The rolling bias is the recent mean of (actual saves − this projection), half-life 30 days, shrunk by 20 starts, added after game script. It is the same number on the slate and in the frozen call.

    What each piece does

    • Team shots against / opponent shots for: recency-weighted per-game SOG, shrunk to league average early on. They replace the old fixed 60/40 blend with fitted weights a and b.
    • Time-in-net share: covers pull risk. Early hooks and injuries are why raw SA/G overstates saves, and this replaces the flat ~12% empty-net haircut.
    • Save%: shrunk toward .900 with a prior of shots, so a hot 10 games doesn't swing the number.
    • Home, playoff and back-to-back: multipliers measured from 2024-25 shot volume.
    • Between seasons: team shot rates keep of last year's weight, because rosters turn over. Early-season numbers lean on the league average and firm up after about 15 games.

    Reading the tables

    • In the saves, points and winners tables (All lines), green rows are OVER and red rows are UNDER.
    • Confirm the starter (morning skate or warmups) before locking. Every projection is for the goalie shown.
    • Overtime inflates raw saves past the per-60 projection. In a tight, high-event series, lean OVER on close calls.
    • Play-by-play extras (GSAx/60, rebound%, block%, CF%, xGF%) are on the Goalies and Teams tabs for reading a matchup. MoneyPuck xGoals, GSAx, unblocked attempts and high-danger rates were tested walk-forward on this season and did not beat the current projection, so they stay out of the formula. The build cross-checks NHL shots, saves and time on ice against MoneyPuck and stops if they diverge.
